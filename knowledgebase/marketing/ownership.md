# Who can help me?

If you have a general marketing question, go to #team-marketing in Slack.

If you need help with the website, go to #posthogdotcom.

Here's a quick guide to who to ask if you want help with a specific marketing activity.

I need a product marketer, but I don't know which

I'm interested in running, attending, or speaking at an event

I want to launch my product out of beta

I need help with documentation

Someone wants to write a guest post / is requesting a backlink etc.

Someone wants to partner with us

Someone wants us to sponsor them

I want to create a video, or have a video idea we should try...

A customer is interested in doing a case study with us

A customer has an issue with merch

I have a question / problem / suggestion for the website

Hey, can we run some paid ads for my product?

I need a new hedgehog design, illustration, or other art asset.

I need a font, logo, etc.

A journalist has contacted me
